## The role

Build OTP services for contract execution, scheduled obligations and workflows between counterparties. The work includes banking and compliance integrations, event sourcing, audit trails, and Phoenix/LiveView interfaces.

Prague-based or willing to relocate, normally with two office days per week. Fully remote is considered for exceptional candidates already in Czechia or the EU. Full-time employment or freelance/B2B contract is available; tell the team which you prefer.

Requires four or more years of backend experience, including at least two years of production Elixir, strong OTP knowledge, Phoenix/Ecto and PostgreSQL. Compensation is discussed as an employment package or contract rate, rather than a published range.
