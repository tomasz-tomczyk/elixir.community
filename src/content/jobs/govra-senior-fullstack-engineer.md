## The role

Build government workflow software alongside founders, customers and product design. Own features across architecture, data models, interfaces and production deployment, with AWS, Terraform and AI-assisted development in the engineering workflow.

Full-time in San Francisco. Requires five or more years of professional engineering experience, including two in a senior role, plus experience shipping early-stage products.

Functional programming and Elixir/Phoenix experience are welcome; the team also considers engineers willing to learn them quickly. Salary is described as competitive with meaningful equity, without a published range. Remote arrangements are not stated.
