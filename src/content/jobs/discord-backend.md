## The role

Work on the Elixir services that deliver messages, presence, and voice state to millions of connected users. You will improve how guild processes scale across the cluster, and you will own a part of the real-time system from design to on-call.

## What you bring

- Several years of backend work, with real production experience in Elixir or Erlang
- A good feel for distributed systems: backpressure, partial failure, and hot spots
- Comfort with profiling and with reading the code of the BEAM itself when you need to
