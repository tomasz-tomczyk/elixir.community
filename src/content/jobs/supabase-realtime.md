## The role

Work on Supabase Realtime, the open-source Elixir server that streams Postgres changes, broadcast, and presence to clients. Most of your work happens in public on GitHub.

## What you bring

- Strong Elixir, with experience in Phoenix Channels or a similar WebSocket system
- Good knowledge of Postgres, ideally logical replication
- Enjoyment of open-source work and talking with users
