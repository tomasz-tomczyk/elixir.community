## How Govra uses Elixir

Govra builds software that turns complex laws and rules into executable workflows for government agencies. Its initial focus is tax administration.

Its full-stack engineering roles include Elixir/Phoenix and functional programming, alongside work on product interfaces, data models and production infrastructure. This is a mixed engineering environment, not a claim that every service is written in Elixir.
