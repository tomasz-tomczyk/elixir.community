## What they do

Fly.io is a cloud platform that runs your app in many regions, close to your users. It runs full virtual machines started from container images.

## How they use Elixir

Fly.io employs Chris McCord, the creator of Phoenix, and funds a lot of Phoenix work. Projects that came out of that work include FLAME, which runs parts of your app on short-lived machines, and Phoenix.new, an AI agent that builds Phoenix apps in the browser.

Fly.io is also a popular place to deploy Elixir. Its private network makes it easy to connect BEAM nodes across regions into one cluster.
