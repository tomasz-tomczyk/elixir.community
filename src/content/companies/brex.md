## What they do

Brex provides corporate cards, spend management, and banking for businesses, from startups to large companies.

## How they use Elixir

Brex built its original backend on Elixir. Payments and card authorisations need many small, fast, concurrent operations, and the BEAM handles that kind of work well.

Over time Brex also adopted other languages for new services. Its early Elixir platform is still a common example of a fintech that started on the BEAM.
