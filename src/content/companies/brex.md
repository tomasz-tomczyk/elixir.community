## How Brex uses Elixir

Brex built much of its early backend in Elixir, including financial business logic and infrastructure for cards. The BEAM provided concurrency and fault tolerance, with PostgreSQL and services deployed on AWS.

Brex moved to Kotlin-first development for new backend services in 2021. Its Elixir work remains a useful adoption story, but this is a historical profile, not a claim that Elixir is its current primary backend language or that it is hiring Elixir engineers.
