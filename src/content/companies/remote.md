## What they do

Remote helps companies hire, pay, and manage people in other countries. It handles local contracts, payroll, taxes, and benefits so a company does not need its own legal entity in each country.

## How they use Elixir

Remote built its product on Elixir and Phoenix from the start and grew to a large engineering team on that stack. Payroll and compliance rules differ in every country, and the team relies on Elixir's clear data flow and pattern matching to keep that logic readable.

Remote is also a well-known Elixir employer. Many of its engineers work fully remote, which fits the company's own product.
