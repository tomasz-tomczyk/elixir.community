## What they do

Pinterest is a visual discovery app. People save and share images ("pins") for ideas such as recipes, home design, and style.

## How they use Elixir

Pinterest rewrote its notification system in Elixir. The new system needed fewer servers and much less code than the system it replaced. The team also released open-source Elixir libraries from that work.

It was one of the first large consumer companies to talk about Elixir in production.
