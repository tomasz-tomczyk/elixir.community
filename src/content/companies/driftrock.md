## How Driftrock uses Elixir

Driftrock builds marketing software for automotive brands. Its engineering team works with Elixir, Phoenix LiveView, PostgreSQL and RabbitMQ.

The work spans lead qualification, customer data and API integrations. RabbitMQ connects services in an event-driven architecture as customer data volumes grow.
