## How Hunit uses Elixir

Hunit turns legal agreements into systems that execute and track their obligations. Elixir/OTP is used for long-running supervised processes, scheduled obligations and workflows involving multiple parties.

The platform integrates banking and external data feeds, captures audit evidence, and uses Phoenix and LiveView for contract authoring, monitoring and human sign-off.
