## What they do

DockYard is a digital product consultancy. It designs and builds web and mobile products for clients.

## How they use Elixir

DockYard builds most of its client work on Elixir and Phoenix. It also invests in open source for the community. It backs LiveView Native, which brings the LiveView model to native iOS and Android apps, and Beacon, a content management system built on Phoenix LiveView.

DockYard staff speak at and sponsor many Elixir conferences.
