## How CyanView uses Elixir

CyanView builds camera-control hardware for live broadcast production. Elixir sits inside its devices, connecting cameras and other video equipment that speak a variety of network protocols.

Binary encoding and decoding helped the team implement those integrations. Its remote control panel coordinated cameras through a custom MQTT protocol, letting operators manage large camera setups from one place.
