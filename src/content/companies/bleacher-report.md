## What they do

Bleacher Report is a sports news site and app. It sends personalised alerts and stories to fans when something happens in a game.

## How they use Elixir

Sports traffic arrives in sharp spikes: a goal or a trade can bring millions of readers within a minute. Bleacher Report moved key parts of its backend from Ruby on Rails to Elixir and Phoenix to handle those spikes.

Their move is one of the most cited early stories of a big team adopting Elixir, because it cut the number of servers they needed by a large amount.
